Frequently asked questions

Do I need to assemble anything?

No. AVERA Stride arrives fully assembled. Take it out of the box, lift the handle until it locks, plug it in and you’re ready to walk – no screws and no tools.

Is it really quiet enough for a flat?

Yes. The 2.5 HP DC motor runs under 45 dB and the 7-layer belt absorbs impact, so you can walk in the evening or during calls without disturbing anyone.

How much space does it need?

In use it measures about 111 × 58 cm. Folded, it is only 15 cm high (130 × 58 × 15 cm) and rolls on built-in wheels, so it fits under most sofas and beds.

What speeds and incline does it offer?

With the handle down it works as a walking pad from 1 to 6 km/h. With the handle up it becomes a treadmill from 1 to 12 km/h. The deck can be set to a 7% incline for uphill walking.

Is it suitable for beginners and older users?

Absolutely. It starts at just 1 km/h, has a sturdy handle to hold, a safety key that stops the belt instantly and a remote so you never need to bend down. It supports users up to 120 kg.

What’s in the box and what about warranty?

You receive the treadmill, remote control, safety key and user manual. Every AVERA Stride comes with a 2-year warranty and our 30-day home trial.
